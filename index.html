Cleaner browsing on FitGirl Repacks.

Hide or blur Adult-tagged listings, and optionally center the layout on wide screens.

Install guide Privacy policy

What it does

One purpose: filter Adult content and tidy the page layout.

  • Hide or blur

    Remove Adult-tagged posts entirely, or soft-blur cover images so they stay out of focus.

  • Grid-aware

    Works on article pages and homepage grid tiles using the site’s Adult tag archive.

  • Center layout

    Optionally center the fixed-width theme on wide viewports from the popup.
