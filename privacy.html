Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-14

What Data We Collect

FG Enhancer stores only the settings you choose in the popup: whether the Adult filter is enabled, hide vs blur mode, and whether to center the page layout. To filter grid tiles, it also builds a list of Adult-tagged page URLs from fitgirl-repacks.site and caches that list locally for a short time (about 6 hours).

The extension does not collect your name, email, browsing history outside that site, payment information, or analytics.

How Data Is Stored

  • Settings use Chrome’s chrome.storage.sync API so they can sync across Chrome browsers signed into the same Google account. That sync is handled by Google, not by us.
  • Adult URL index is stored only in chrome.storage.local on your device and is refreshed periodically from fitgirl-repacks.site.

We do not operate our own servers and do not receive your data.

How Data Is Used

Settings control the filter and layout features. The Adult URL index is used only to identify which listings on fitgirl-repacks.site should be hidden or blurred.

Third-Party Services

The extension does not use analytics, advertising, or other third-party tracking services. It only contacts fitgirl-repacks.site to load the Adult tag pages needed for filtering, and relies on Google Chrome sync if you use a signed-in Chrome profile.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share user data with third parties. Settings synced via Chrome Sync are subject to Google’s Chrome Privacy Notice.

Data Retention and Deletion

Settings remain until you change them, reset to defaults, or uninstall the extension. The Adult URL cache is overwritten on refresh and removed when you uninstall the extension. You can also clear extension storage from Chrome’s site/extension settings.

Changes to This Policy

If data practices change, this page will be updated with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact

For privacy questions about FG Enhancer, open an issue on the GitHub repository or contact the publisher email listed on the Chrome Web Store listing.
